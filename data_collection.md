## Portwatch

PortWatch is an open-source platform developed by the IMF and the University of Oxford that tracks maritime trade in near real-time. It primarily utilizes Automatic Identification System (AIS) data, which consists of satellite-captured signals from over 95,000 ships worldwide, to create several layers of economic and logistics data.

## Daily Port Activity

Portwatch gathers data of daily port activity in 26 Phillippine ports using satellite AIS signals.  

## Relevant Features

1. **Daily Vessel Traffic (Port Calls)** - PortWatch tracks the physical presence of ships at a port on a daily basis. A "port call" is recorded when a vessel enters a predefined geofenced area (the port boundary).
2. **Daily Trade Volume Estimates (Shipments)** - Unlike official customs data, which measures trade in dollar value, PortWatch estimates the physical weight of the goods being moved.
