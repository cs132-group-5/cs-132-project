Hi. We are the ABCs of Data Science (Anika, Bea, & Carl).

And this is our project, Ship Happens: A Comprehensive Study on Port Activity in the Philippines. This study aims to identify the busiest ports in the Philippines, understand trends in port activity from 2019 to 2025, and develop a predictive model for shipping activity.

Here's an overview of our project.

Analyzing port activity to promote sustainable consumption and resource efficiency.

Motivation

Aligned with SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production), this project focuses on waste reduction, sustainable consumption, and resource efficiency in maritime logistics.

Problem

What are the busiest ports and when is traffic highest? We hypothesize that Manila is the busiest, peaking during the Christmas season and causing national delays.

Solution

By identifying busy ports, understanding trends from 2019-2025, and creating a predictive model, we aim to optimize shipping schedules and resource allocation.

We explored PortWatch's daily port activity data.

Source

PortWatch via Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX).

Daily Port Activity

Philippine Daily Port Activity and Shipment Estimates.

Period

Daily data from January 1, 2019 to November 28, 2025.

Relevant features of the Portwatch data

Daily Vessel Traffic

Are counted as "port calls" when a vessel enters a predefined geofenced area (the port boundary).

Daily Trade Volume Estimates

The volume of import and export shipments in each of the ports are estimated in metric tons.

Here's what we found out.

Manila has the greatest port activity

Looking at total cargo volume from 2019 to 2025 across all ports, Manila far exceeds the other ports.

Port activity peaks during August

Across all ports, total import and export cargo peaks during August.

We hypothesised that the busiest shipping port in the Philippines is Manila, and port activity peaks during the Christmas season (November-December) causing shipping delays throughout the country because of the higher demand.

Port activity is greatest in Manila during the month of August

Across all ports in the Phillippines, Manila has the greatest port activity, and port activity peaks during the month of August, not November-December as we hypothesized.
